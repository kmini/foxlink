FoxLink Support

Email hukai0663@qq.com for help with FoxLink on iPhone and iPad, purchases, or desktop connections.

Common issues

  • Tracking does not start. Check FoxLink’s Camera permission in Settings and calibrate with your face pointed at the screen.
  • Your desktop app is not receiving data. Check FoxLink’s Local Network permission and confirm that both devices are on the same network. Make sure the desktop app is listening on the configured address and port.
  • Pro is missing after switching devices. Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought Pro, then open the Pro screen in FoxLink and select Restore Purchases.

When writing for help, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, FoxLink version from About, and which action did not work. For connection issues, include the name and version of the receiving app.

狐LINK 技术支持

遇到 iPhone 或 iPad 版狐LINK、购买或桌面连接问题，请发邮件到 hukai0663@qq.com。

常见问题

  • 面捕无法启动。 在系统设置中确认狐LINK 已获相机权限。面朝屏幕，再使用 App 内的面部校准。
  • 桌面软件收不到数据。 确认狐LINK 已获本地网络权限，两台设备在同一个网络。检查桌面软件是否正在监听你填写的地址和端口。
  • 换设备后找不到 Pro。 使用购买 Pro 的 Apple 账户登录 App Store，再打开狐LINK 的 Pro 页面，选择「恢复购买」。

来信时请附上设备型号、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本、「关于」页里的狐LINK 版本，以及出问题的操作步骤。如果遇到连接问题，请同时写上接收软件的名称和版本。
