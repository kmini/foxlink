FoxLink Privacy Policy

This policy covers FoxLink for iPhone and iPad. Effective November 26, 2026.

Camera, microphone, and imported files

FoxLink uses your camera to track face and head movement. If you let FoxLink use the microphone, it can use audio for lip sync or send audio when you enable it in an NDI® stream. Tracking and lip-sync processing run on your device. FoxLink does not upload camera frames, microphone recordings, or face tracking data to a developer-operated server.

FoxLink stores imported avatars and mappings, connection settings, and pairing information on your device so you can use them again.

Network connections you enable

When you enable output or pair with desktop software, FoxLink can send tracking values, avatar motion, commands, or a rendered avatar stream to the receivers you configure. NDI streams can be discoverable on the local network. Enable NDI audio only if you intend to send microphone audio to receivers. Receiving apps, including third-party software, handle the data they receive under their own privacy practices.

Purchases and support

Apple processes App Store purchases. FoxLink uses StoreKit to check and restore your purchase entitlement. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

If you email support, we use your email address and the details you provide to handle your request. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle the request, follow up, or meet legal obligations. Please omit avatar files, camera images, or recordings unless they are necessary to resolve the issue. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Permissions and deletion

You can disable the camera, microphone, or local network permission in Settings and stop network outputs in FoxLink. Remove imported items from their respective screens. Deleting FoxLink removes its local files. To ask about deleting information you sent to support, contact hukai0663@qq.com.

狐LINK 隐私政策

本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 版狐LINK，自 2026 年 11 月 26 日生效。

相机、麦克风和导入文件

狐LINK 使用相机跟踪面部和头部动作。允许使用麦克风后，App 可以根据声音驱动口型，也可以在你开启 NDI® 音频时发送麦克风声音。面捕和口型分析在设备上完成。狐LINK 不向开发者运营的服务器上传相机画面、麦克风录音或面捕数据。

你导入的模型和映射、连接设置及配对信息保存在设备上，供下次使用。

由你开启的网络连接

当你开启输出或配对桌面软件时，狐LINK 可向你配置的接收端发送面捕参数、模型动作、控制指令或渲染后的模型画面。局域网中的接收端可能会发现 NDI 串流。只有在你打算让接收端收到麦克风声音时，才开启 NDI 音频。接收端软件依其各自的隐私规则处理收到的数据。

购买及技术支持

App Store 购买由 Apple 处理。狐LINK 使用 StoreKit 查询和恢复购买权益。开发者不会收到你的银行卡资料。

如果你发送支持邮件，我们会使用你的邮箱和你提供的资料来处理请求。我们在处理请求、跟进问题或履行法定义务所需的期间内保留支持邮件。除非排查问题确有需要，请勿附上模型文件、相机画面或录音。本站由 GitHub Pages 托管，网站访问适用 GitHub 隐私声明。

权限和删除

你可以在系统设置中关闭相机、麦克风或本地网络权限，也可以在狐LINK 中关闭各项网络输出。导入的文件可在相应界面移除。删除 App 会移除其本地文件。如果你希望删除此前发给技术支持的资料，请联系 hukai0663@qq.com。
